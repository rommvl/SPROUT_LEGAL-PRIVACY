1. Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Sprout app ("Sprout", "the app") uses and why. "I", "me", and "my" mean the individual developer of Sprout.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your pet and save data: your pet's stats, items, coins, paintings, settings, and progress are saved only on your iPhone. I can't see them.
  • Chats with Sprout: by default, everything you type to Sprout is handled by an AI model that runs on your phone. Your messages are not sent to me or stored on any server of mine.

Data I collect: none

Sprout does not collect, store, or send me any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics tools, no tracking, and no ads right now. I have no server that receives data from the app.

Everything Sprout uses

To be fully open, here is every outside service, tool, and piece of software Sprout uses, and whether it sees anything about you:

  • Apple App Store & TestFlight (Apple Inc.): how you download and update Sprout. Apple handles your Apple ID, downloads, and any payments under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple gives me only anonymous totals (like download counts), never who you are.
  • Apple crash reports (optional): only if you've turned on Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers. These reports are anonymous. If that setting is off, I get nothing.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): the first time you open Sprout, or if you pick a different brain in Settings, the app downloads the AI model file from Hugging Face. Like any download, Hugging Face's servers can see your IP address and basic device and network info for that request, under Hugging Face's Privacy Policy. Nothing about your pet or your chats is sent.
  • Qwen AI model (open-source model by Alibaba's Qwen team): this is Sprout's "brain". It runs entirely on your phone and does not connect to the internet.
  • NobodyWho (open-source plugin): the code that runs the AI model on your phone. It works offline and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Godot Engine (open-source game engine): what Sprout is built with. It does not collect data.
  • Noto Color Emoji (open-source font by Google): included inside the app to draw emoji. It's a file in the app, not an online service, and sends nothing to Google.
  • Optional outside AI service: Sprout's settings let you connect your own outside AI service instead of the on-device brain. This is off by default. If you turn it on, the messages you send go to the service you chose, under its privacy policy.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): where I store Sprout's code and build the app. It's used only by me and never receives anything from your phone.

Ads and coin purchases (not active yet)

In the future, Sprout may show a small number of ads and offer optional coin purchases. Neither is turned on right now. Before either one is added, I will update this page to name the exact companies involved and explain what they collect. If ads are added, Apple's "Allow Tracking" prompt will let you decide whether ads can be personalized, and coin purchases will always be handled by Apple, so I never see your payment details.

Emailing me

If you email me, I'll see your email address and whatever you write. I'll use it only to reply and won't share it.

What I never do

  • I don't sell or share your personal information.
  • I don't ask you to create an account.
  • I don't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
  • I don't track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: deleting the Sprout app removes your pet, chats, and save data from your phone. Because I don't keep a copy, this can't be undone.
  • Questions or requests: you can contact me about anything in this policy.

Age

Sprout is meant for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent me personal information (for example by email), contact me and I'll delete it.

Security

Because your data stays on your device, it's protected by your iPhone's own security (passcode, Face ID, encryption). No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and up to date.

Changes to this policy

If Sprout changes (for example, if ads, purchases, or any new service are added), I'll update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top. Continuing to use Sprout after a change means you accept the updated policy.
